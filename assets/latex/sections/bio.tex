\begin{rSection}{Bio}
    {\small
        Dr. Zhaomin Wu is an NRF Postdoctoral Fellow and Research Fellow at the Department of Computer Science, National University of Singapore. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from NUS in 2024, advised by Prof. Bingsheng He. His research focuses on trustworthy AI, federated learning, privacy-preserving machine learning, LLM safety, multi-agent systems, and data management for collaborative learning.

        He received the Singapore NRF Postdoctoral Award in 2026, a highly competitive national-level fellowship awarded by the National Research Foundation, Singapore, providing four-year salary support and an independent SGD 250{,}000 research grant. His work has been recognized with two ICLR 2026 Oral papers, each selected as top 1\% of submissions, the SIGMOD 2023 Honorable Mention for Best Artifact, the NUS School of Computing Honorable Mention for Best Ph.D. Thesis Award, and the NUS Institute of Data Science Best Research Staff Award. As of May 2026, his work has received 2{,}710 citations on Google Scholar.
    }
\end{rSection}
