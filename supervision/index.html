Current openings

I have openings for Postdocs, Ph.D. students, and Research Assistants (RAs) at Hong Kong Baptist University.

How to apply →

My guiding principles

  • A professor's success is measured primarily by their students' success, beyond publications and grants.
  • Students work with their supervisors, not for them, as partners in research.

What I look for

Basic requirements

  • Location: Work onsite at HKBU.

  • English: Professional work efficiency (HKBU admission requirements).

  • Skills: Solid foundations in programming, mathematics, systems, machine learning, and algorithms.

Responsible
Honesty and reliability in honoring commitments.
Proactive
Initiative in seeking advisors' input and sharing updates.
Persistent
Resilience through setbacks and openness to advice.
Curious
An eagerness to explore the unknown and understand why.

How to apply

zhaomin@hkbu.edu.hk

Please email me your CV, academic transcript, and a research description (under 100 words). Include your research area, intended start date, and expected duration in the email.

Subject format: HKBU [Position] Application - [Name]. Use PhD, RA, or Postdoc for the position.

Example: HKBU PhD Application - Alex Chen

In your research description, briefly address:

  1. Briefly describe your research background (if any) and your favorite work (a paper, thesis, or project).
  2. Why would you like to work with me?
  3. Which of my research directions or papers connects most closely with your interests?
